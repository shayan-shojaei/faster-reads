# Code of Conduct

Faster Reads contributors are expected to make participation respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

Examples of acceptable behavior include constructive feedback, empathy, clear disagreement about ideas rather than people, and respect for others’ privacy. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, or publishing private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates these standards. Serious or sensitive incidents may be reported privately through the repository’s security-advisory channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
