Welcome to Faster Reads

Guide your eyes through dense text.

Faster Reads adds weight to the beginning of words in sections you choose, while preserving the page around them.

Reading longer articles can feel lighter when each word offers a visual anchor.

Make it yours

Choose your reading intensity.

Your choice is saved with Chrome Sync and can be changed any time in settings.

You stay in control

Select only what you want to change.

  1. 1
    Open any webpage

    Click the Faster Reads toolbar icon or press Alt + Shift + B.

  2. 2
    Choose a section

    Hover to preview, then click to save it. Press Escape to cancel.

  3. 3
    Allow that site

    Optional site access lets the saved emphasis return on later visits.
